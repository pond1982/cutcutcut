Timeline

From the first night to the first testers

Day by day, in the order it happened. Every entry is a real commit or a real upload. The game began on Day 1 as "Samurai Fight Fight".

Day 1

Concept

The idea was small: a lone samurai, an office tower, one room after another. It was playable the same night under the working title Samurai Fight Fight.

  1. First playable

    An isometric zombie tower crawl in Unreal Engine 5.8. No level file: the game builds each floor in code when you arrive.

    Read the post
  2. The ronin girl becomes the main character

    Rin replaces the placeholder samurai.

  3. Furnished floors and furniture physics

    Desks and chairs that get in the way and get knocked over, plus the first showcase and phone recording.

Day 2

A level, not a room

Floor 1 stops being a test room and becomes a place with staff, a PA system, a boss and a comic-book look.

  1. Floor 1 as a full level

    Falcifer's arena, staff zombies, PA announcements, drops, and the thick-ink comic look.

    Read the post
  2. Rin's run follows her speed

    Isometric furniture sets fill the rooms.

Day 3

The name and the tower

The longest day. The game gets its real name, its running joke, its plan to release, and most of its hundred floors.

  1. The Clinic, floors 21 to 40

    The stairwell, Hana's texts between floors, and a fall with a retry.

  2. Homura, the rival blade

    And a board of floors for jumping around the tower in testing.

  3. Subject 001, the Clinic's Elder

    Homura cuts into the fight on floor 30.

  4. It is called Cut Cut Cut

    The title screen, the Collector, a fighting style for each boss, and the z-word joke.

    Read the post
  5. First boss moves to floor 5

    Floors 1 to 4 stay plain so the rhythm builds before a guardian breaks it.

  6. The Desk grows up

    Progression, controls, encounters and environment passes for floors 1 to 20.

  7. The Expedition arrives

    Roaming squads, tech recovery rewards and localized lighting.

  8. Rin's deck is designed on paper

    A simulated paper playtest before any card code.

    Read the post
  9. The Residences

    Floors 41 to 55, the maintenance chief, the Envoy's choice at the tower core, and the Chair of the Assembly.

  10. The painted deck, the portraits and two garden endings

Day 4

Floor 100

The climb is finished end to end just after midnight, then balanced, filmed, narrated in Thai, taught to new players and packaged for Android.

  1. The whole climb is playable

    Story, deck, two rooftop endings and Endless mode.

  2. Ophiel at the tower core

    Every route to dawn verified by automated climbs.

  3. The balance pass

    The full climb balanced, and each guardian given a distinct fight.

  4. The showcase, narrated in Thai

    Voiceover and synced subtitles with ElevenLabs v4, then a developer update in Pondd's own voice.

    Read the post
  5. Rin's Ultimate: the ink kick

    She kicks the blade across the room. Filmed, then reworked twice the same afternoon.

    Read the post
  6. The first fight teaches the game

    A sharper iPhone view and a first room that teaches slash, combo, roll and Ultimate.

    Read the post
  7. Android landscape playtest

    Packaged and optimized for a first Android test.

  6. This site goes up

Day 6

A tester, a finisher, and a long evening

The build that never reached TestFlight finally does, the first tester writes back, and one boss's ending becomes six shared by nearly all of them. Then the evening: the guardians' rooms are dressed, Galahad is rebuilt from a drawing, and the ultimate gets an impact frame.

  1. Really on TestFlight

    Two uploads were dropped by Apple without a word on screen. The third is there, and was checked.

  2. A boss cut scene

    Six guardians and the ultimate, narrated.

  3. The first tester's notes

    A longer roll, softer cuts, and a flick that rolls while holding to cut.

  4. The Porter's own ending

    A finish call, a launch, thirteen cuts in slowed time, and a line about delivery.

    Read the post
  5. Six shared endings

    Every guardian but one gets a finish call, six endings dealt at random, and a PA that shouts FINALISE HIM!

    Read the post
  6. The endings rebuilt

    A launcher, an air cut, a dive and a real sheathing, all from clips that were already in the project.

    Read the post
  7. A sharper phone

    The newest iPhone had been drawing a quarter of its pixels. The setting is raised; not yet measured on a phone.

  8. Full rooms

    Every guardian's room dressed to its story: wall pieces, signs, litter and a marking on the floor. No fight is changed.

    Read the post
  9. Galahad, from a drawing

    His own body and moves, and a shield that took four tries to become an oval of light over his hand.

    Read the post
  10. One black frame

    An impact frame on the ultimate's kick, one of ten at random, and a landing that no longer leans back.

  11. Sparks, steam and butterflies

    An effects pack in the guardians' rooms and at the moments of their fights.

Day 7

The Envoy, and every guardian in phrases

Sounds go in, a size check finds the last build was missing its new art, and the Envoy is rebuilt four times before the real reason his body vanished turns up. Then every guardian's fight is made of short mixed phrases, and new builds go to TestFlight and Android.

  1. Fourteen sounds

    Gunfire, a drawn weapon, explosions and a roar, in under five megabytes.

  2. A size check

    Most of the app is the engine and a few art packs. The last build was missing Galahad and the effects; the pack list is fixed.

  3. Why the Envoy vanished

    A recoil clip played as a whole pose drew his body as a single point for every volley.

    Read the post
  4. Every guardian in phrases

    27 new floor attacks, mixed into short runs, never the same one twice.

  5. New builds

    On TestFlight, marked valid by Apple, and an Android build.

  6. Every sound from ElevenLabs

    455 effects and beds, 20 pieces of music and 745 voiced lines, all from the lore.

    Read the post
  7. Weapons, feet and slow motion

    Each guardian's weapon heard through its fight, footsteps by floor, the finish call's slow motion slowing the sound.

  8. Films with sound

    The game's films record their sound frame by frame, in step with the picture.

  9. Music turned down

    The owner couldn't hear the lines: every piece now steps back when someone speaks.

  10. New build

    Uploaded to TestFlight.

Day 8

Looking for players

A list of where testers gather, the trailer on TikTok, and an itch.io page with the Android build, waiting to go public.

  1. A new Android build

    With all of Day 7's sound. The first try failed on an emptied cook folder.

  2. Ten places to find players

    Ranked by how well each fits a mobile action game, with drafts for each.

    Read the post
  3. The trailer on TikTok

    In English, labelled as AI-generated and as the owner's own product.

  4. An itch.io page

    AI-made cover and banner, real screenshots, and the 496 MB Android build pushed with butler. Still a draft.
