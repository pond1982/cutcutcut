It is called Cut Cut Cut

The game got its real name, a title screen, a reason for the tower, a running joke about the word "zombie", and a plan that runs all the way to release.

Day 3
NameStoryRoadmap

The name

"Samurai Fight Fight" was a folder name. Cut Cut Cut names what the player does. The logo writes it CUT/CUT/CUT; the slashes are for the logo only. The store name adds 100 Floors of Zombies so it can be found, and so nobody mistakes it for a video editor.

Five title screens were painted. We picked the first: Rin standing before the tower with her aura up.

The story

The tower is Hōrai Tower, built by a longevity company that sold Eternal Residency: Live forever. Never leave. On the night of its gala the founder released the final treatment through the vents. Nobody inside can die now. Rin was the bodyguard of the founder's daughter, dismissed a week earlier for objecting. Then one text arrived: Come up. Please.

The tone is deliberate. The story underneath is earnest. Everything on the surface is funny. Rin is the straight man.

The z-word

"Zombie" is a rude word. They prefer "undying".

Rin keeps calling them zombies. The residents consider it politically incorrect, and each objects in its own voice: Compliance cites policy, the gardener says it is what the weeds call them. Rin has a comeback every time. It runs through every boss's lines.

The plan

The roadmap was written today with release gates instead of dates. The decisions that are settled: free to download with floors 1 to 20 free, one purchase for the rest; iPhone first, in landscape; all 100 floors; a story climb, then an endless mode.

Built the same day

  • The Clinic, floors 21 to 40, with the stairwell and Hana's texts between floors.
  • Homura, the Expedition's rival blade, and Subject 001, the Clinic's Elder.
  • The Collector, a trophy hunter with 20 mounted heads on his walls.
  • A different fighting style for each boss.
  • The first boss moved from floor 1 to floor 5, so the rhythm builds before a guardian breaks it.
