Homura learns the Captain's forms

The Expedition's duellist now fights with eight real sword forms across three duels that get faster and harder. She escapes the first two. Her blade glows red.

17 November 202612:55
BossesCombatDay 5

Homura wants Rin's sword, because her own thermal blade only cauterises the undying. She meets Rin three times: floors 30, 65 and 100.

Eight forms

She now fights as the Captain does in Ink & Legend, form for form: Falling Mountain, Three Steps, Swallow's Return, Still Water, One Flash, Tempest, Secret Art, Heaven and Earth. Between forms she guards, pauses and steps.

Cut into her still water, into a set piece's gathering, or a third time in a row at her waiting guard, and the cut is turned aside and answered.

Three duels, each harder

Each meeting is quicker, more pressing and harder-hitting than the last. On 30 and 65 she is not cut down: the finishing cut is turned aside and she escapes through the far door. On 100 she lays her blade down.

The red blade

Her katana carries a red beam with a shell of light, a glow on the floor beneath it, and a fading streak when swung.

Credits where due

Thirteen of the long sword and sabre clips come from KCISA's motion capture of Joseon martial arts (CC BY 4.0), retargeted onto her skeleton. They are credited on the game's credits page.

Not checked yet

Nobody has played these duels by hand. The numbers are first guesses.
