The whole tower: 100 floors, two endings, Endless

Fifteen minutes past midnight the climb was playable from the lobby to the rooftop garden, with a choice at the core, two endings and an endless mode. Then it was balanced.

Day 4
MilestoneCampaign

What is in the climb

  • The Desk (1 to 20), the Clinic (21 to 40), the Residences (41 to 70), Sky Amenities (71 to 90), the Gala (91 to 99) and the Garden on 100.
  • A guardian on every fifth floor, and an extra one at the Gala.
  • The tower core on floor 50, where the Expedition's Envoy offers a deal. Opening or restoring the lockdown changes the routes above.
  • Two rooftop endings, each illustrated and scored, then the credits.
  • Endless, unlocked from the title once the story is done.

Verified by climbing it

The game can climb itself. Scripted climbs ran to both endings from saved loadouts, checked that an Endless death never touches the story save, and swept thousands of generated floor plans for blocked routes.

The balance pass

By the morning the full climb had been balanced and every guardian had its own fight. The start-to-credits film below is one automated climb after that pass.

What this does not mean

It means the tower exists and can be finished. It does not mean it is fun on every floor. No human has yet climbed it unassisted on a phone.
