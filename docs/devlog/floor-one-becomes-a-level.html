Floor 1 becomes a level

The first floor stopped being a test room. It got staff, a PA system, drops, a boss with a title card, and the thick-ink comic look the whole game now wears.

Day 2
Art directionBosses

A room full of enemies is a test. A level has people in it who were doing something before you walked in.

The staff

The undying on the first floors are Hōrai Life's office staff. They stayed late for the gala and they are still at work. The PA still makes announcements. Things drop when they fall.

The first boss: Falcifer

The first full boss is the tower's gardener: Falcifer, Head of Landscaping. Hooded and faceless under a mantle of thorns, carrying a sickle that drips green sap. Its subtitle on the card reads Composted Once. Came Back Perennial.

Bosses are introduced the way Borderlands does it, and fought with scripted beats and banter. Falcifer talks of pruning, mulch and bone meal. Vines burst out of the floor where it points, and leave sap Rin has to wade through.

The look

This is the day the game found its look: comic-book ink, thick outlines, strong silhouettes, title cards in cut-out lettering. Everything painted since follows it.

Also today

Rin's run now follows her actual speed, and the rooms are furnished from an isometric interiors set.
