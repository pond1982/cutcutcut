I want the first room of CUT/CUT/CUT to teach you how to survive the next 99.

How the lesson works

Five clerks meet Rin in the floor-one lobby. The fight pauses while you try a slash, a marked Combo, a directional swipe to roll, and an Ultimate that clears the room. Skip lesson releases the clerks into ordinary combat.

If you missed the roll, How to Play on the title or pause menu lets you practise it. Continue an older climb past floor one without having learned it, and the room pauses once for a practice swipe.
