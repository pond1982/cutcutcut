Build 0.1 is on TestFlight

The first build reached Apple's testing service. It is one tester for now: the developer, on a new phone.

Day 5
iPhoneTestFlight

The upload finished with two words: Upload succeeded.

What it took

  • A Shipping build, signed for distribution and archived, made by one script.
  • An app record on App Store Connect, which Xcode created.
  • Two false starts: a script that could not find the archive it had just made, and an error dialog from Xcode that turned out to be Xcode checking for the new app record a few seconds too early.

Who can play it

One person. TestFlight is where the roadmap's beta gates will happen later: first a closed group of friends (can they finish the free section, and where do they stop), then an open one for balance, crashes and device spread.

What it is for right now

Three questions no automated climb can answer: is the frame rate good on a real phone, are the touch controls comfortable, and does the first guardian on floor 5 feel fair to someone who is not a script?
