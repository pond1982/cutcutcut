Every sound, from ElevenLabs

The owner asked for all of the game's sound to be made with ElevenLabs, in keeping with the lore. By the evening the tower had music, voices for every character, and a sound for nearly everything that happens in it, and films of the game had sound for the first time.

Day 7
SoundMusicVoicesBuildsVideo

The morning's fourteen sounds were a start. In the evening the owner asked for the whole job: every sound in the game made with ElevenLabs, music, dialogue, room sound and effects, and the borrowed sounds replaced. A sound list written two days earlier said what the game needed. Then the owner added a rule that shaped the rest of it: keep a strong theme from the lore, from what kind of game this is, and from how it looks.

What was made

  • 455 sound effects, loops and room beds.
  • 20 pieces of music.
  • 745 spoken lines, in 26 voices.

That came to about 73,000 ElevenLabs credits. Three helper agents worked through the code at the same time, one on Rin and the undying, one on the guardians and one on the tower and the menus. Each listed every sound it hooked in, with a prompt for what it should sound like. One generator made them all, and running it again remakes only what has changed.

Sounds from the lore

Rin's sword cuts the undying and they bleed ink, so her cuts are steel and a wet splash of ink, not blood. The undying are the tower's own staff and residents, still at work: a groan with an office sigh in it. The tower is a building still running on emergency power after the end, with humming lights and a cheerful corporate chime. The Expedition is a different world: suppressed rifles, radio squelch and the rip of a strap.

Every guardian's sounds come from who they are:

  • Whisper's rubber stamp and desk phone.
  • The Chair's division bell.
  • The Porter's runaway trolley.
  • The Pacer's whistle.
  • The Conductor's baton.
  • The Sun Messiah's humming lamps.

Each guardian's floor attack now has a warning sound and a landing sound. The cards and menus are paper, brush and wood block. Results get a big comic stamp, in the same spirit as the boss cards.

The music

The music is a piece for each part of the tower, plus pieces for its big moments, and every prompt was written from the lore:

  • The Desk: office music gone slightly wrong.
  • The Clinic: a heart monitor in the beat.
  • The Residences: a faded city-pop groove through apartment walls.
  • The Gala: a waltz played by an orchestra that cannot stop.
  • Fights and moments: the title, the guardian fights, a colder machine theme for the Expedition's own guardians, Homura's duels, the stairwell and endless mode.

A first set was made before the lore rule. It was all made again.

Voices

Every line in the dialogue box and on the PA is now spoken. Each speaker got a voice of its own:

  • Rin keeps the voice from the intro film.
  • Whisper whispers.
  • Galahad speaks through his frame's speaker.
  • The Expedition comes over a radio.
  • The building's announcements come through ceiling speakers, chime first.

A line stays on screen until it has been said. If a line's words change later, it goes quiet until it is voiced again, rather than playing the old words.

Weapons, feet and slow motion

The owner then asked for three more:

  • Weapons: each guardian's weapon is heard all through its fight, louder as it comes close. Homura's blade hums with heat, the Chief's keys jangle and the Pacer's stopwatch ticks. Rin's own sword hums faintly, more when the ultimate is ready.
  • Feet: footsteps follow the floor: office carpet, clinic linoleum, parquet, marble at the Gala, gravel in the garden.
  • Slow motion: when the finish call slows time, the world's sound slows with it, deeper and muffled, under a heartbeat, while Rin's own sounds keep their speed. This was an owner's note from the day before.

Films with sound

Until today every film of the game was silent. The game films itself one frame at a time, far slower than real time, so sound recorded as it played would run ahead of the picture. Unreal has a mixer that renders sound only when asked. Each frame now asks for exactly its thirtieth of a second, and the two stay together. The first try made three times too much sound, because the mixer was also rendering on its own every frame. That was found and switched off.

Too loud

The owner watched the films and could not hear the dialogue over the music. Only the section music had been stepping back when someone spoke. The fight themes played on at full level. Now all music drops to a third under a line, the music is a quarter to a third quieter overall, and the voices and the PA are louder.

A new build

A new build with all of this has gone to TestFlight.

What has not been checked

  • Nobody has listened to most of it. The sounds were chosen by length and loudness, not by ear. Some takes will be wrong for their moment.
  • The new balance between music and voices has not been heard.
  • Whether busy rooms pile sounds up has only been reasoned about.
  • Whether the music loops join without a seam has not been heard.
  • Whether the slow-motion sound plays in a real finish has not been heard.
  • The phone build is larger with this much sound, and has not been opened on a phone.
  • In the fights run without a picture, with sound switched off, no sound was missing and no line went unvoiced.
