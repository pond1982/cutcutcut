The showcase, narrated in Thai

Two films for the Thai developer community: the landscape showcase with a Thai voiceover, and a developer update told in Pondd's own voice.

Day 4
VideoThaiVoice

Cut Cut Cut is made in Thailand, and the first people we wanted to show it to speak Thai.

The showcase

The script was written to the film, one paragraph per shot, 37 shots. It was voiced with ElevenLabs v4 and given synced subtitles.

The developer update

The second film tells the same footage as a developer update, in Pondd's own cloned voice, for the Thai AI Dev group on Facebook. The post that went with it invited early mobile playtest feedback.

What we learned

Writing narration to shot timings first, then voicing it, is far easier than cutting pictures to a finished voice track. We did it the same way again for the English intro.
