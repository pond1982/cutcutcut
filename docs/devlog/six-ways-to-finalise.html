Six ways to finalise a guardian

This morning one boss had an ending of his own. By the afternoon every guardian but one shares six, dealt at random, and the building's PA has something to shout.

Day 6
CombatBossesVideo

This morning's post ended with one guardian, the Freight Porter, getting an ending of his own. It was a lot of work for one boss out of fifteen. So the owner made a call that changed the plan: have a handful of endings and rotate them at random, instead of making one for each boss.

That is what the game does now. Every guardian but one gets the finish call, and answering it in time deals one of six endings, never the same twice running.

The six

1. Diced. Thrown into the air, hung there, cut thirteen times while the world runs at a twelfth of its speed, and then in pieces. 2. Bisected. The opposite: one cut in stopped time. She lowers the sword. Then he slides apart. 3. Launched. Knocked limp across the room. She is after him at once, and the cut lands where he fetches up. 4. Spiked. Thrown up, met in the air, and driven back down into the floor. 5. Stamped. No cut at all. She taps him, the PA approves the paperwork, and a red stamp comes down out of the sky. 6. Pinned. She throws her sword through him into the far wall, walks over in no hurry, and takes it back.

The one guardian left out is Homura. She is never cut down by a finisher, and the reason is not for this page.

What the PA shouts

Everyone knows the cry from fighting games. The owner wanted it, as a joke, in the tower's own voice and not copied. The tower is an office. So as the guardian drops, the building's PA announces:

FINALISE HIM!

It is the same voice that has been cancelling appointments since floor 1.

What the owner's notes changed

The ending went through a dozen takes today. The notes that shaped it, in short:

  • The camera view was blocked. The overhead camera now comes down behind her shoulder for the call.
  • The title was blocking the view. The title moved from the middle of the screen to a band across the top.
  • Why does the camera shake? Because one had been added to every hit. They are gone.
  • He should fall, but upward. In the air he is no longer a posed figure. He goes limp with gravity switched off and is pulled up by the hips, so his limbs trail.
  • More slow motion, with her still fast. The world drops to a twelfth of its speed. She alone keeps hers.
  • Too many camera cuts. Nine shots in eight seconds became four shots and two cuts.

What has not been checked

  • Only the Porter has been filmed. The six endings run on him. The other fourteen guardians have different bodies, and cutting one apart at the joints, or making it go limp, may not work cleanly on all of them. Each needs a look.
  • Nobody has answered a finish call by hand. The swipes in the film are the game's own bot.
  • She does not lift anyone, and the sword never goes into a scabbard. There are no clips for either.
  • The automated tests that need no special setup pass. The boss tests that end a fight with a single tap were not re-run, and a tap no longer ends one.
  • The video was checked from stills, not watched in motion, by the agent that built it.
  • None of this is in the TestFlight build yet.
