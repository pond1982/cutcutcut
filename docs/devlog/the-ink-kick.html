Rin's Ultimate: she kicks the sword

The Ultimate needed to feel like the end of an argument. Rin kicks her katana across the room. It took three passes in one afternoon to get it right.

16 November 202615:24
CombatAnimationDay 4

Pass one, 15:24

A blade-kick Ultimate, filmed.

Pass two, 16:28

Two changes: the final stroke is held in darkness, and the blood is grounded on the floor instead of hanging in the air.

Pass three, 17:12

The steel cuts across the room were brought back, randomized, so no two Ultimates draw the same lines. The camera was reframed so the whole kick is in shot.

One more fix, 20:10

Rin was turning away from her own kick as she landed. She now keeps facing it through the landing.
