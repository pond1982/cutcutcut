## The Maintenance Chief

The floor-5 guardian was a zombie with a wrench. We tried building a mechanic from an open human generator first. It worked, and he looked like nobody. A ready-made free model with character won: **Bubba The Handyman** by Paul N. Denim overalls, plaid shirt, tool belt, cap.

A Blender script renamed his bones, cleared the animation that kept re-posing him, and carried him onto the zombie skeleton so the fight's existing clips play on him.

::img shot-floor5 | The Chief's card on floor 5.

The wrench took three rounds. Attached at the wrist, it floated beside his hand. It is now placed from his finger joints, so it sits in his fist.

## The night nurse who did not work

For the Clinic we tried a zombie nurse model. Standing still, she was right. Moving on the shared zombie clips, her arms twisted.

## The Winter Widow

So the ward went to someone who already moved well: **the Winter Widow**, from Pondd's other game, Ink & Legend. Recast in white as the Clinic's night nurse. Her subtitle: *Yes, We Have a Sexy Nurse Too*.

She is not a widow herself. She makes them. And she has ice.

::video winter-widow | The designed fight.

Her first version chased Rin and swung, and on film she kept running into nothing. So the fight was written as a pattern first:

- **Rounds.** She circles Rin and stops to watch.
- **Bedside manner.** Close in: a thrust, feints, a hesitation. Only the real cut glints. Then a backflip, and the landing is your opening.
- **Frost prescription.** At range, frost a step ahead of where you are going.
- **Ice lunge.** A crouch, a warning, a dart along a fixed line. Roll aside and she skids to a stop.
- **Cold compress.** Get frozen and she walks up, unhurried, for a heavy cut.
- **Ice ring.** In her second phase, stay close too long and frost bursts around her.
