The dev log now has a video version. It is one minute and fifty-two seconds, it is vertical, and it covers three things: what Cut Cut Cut is, where it stands after five days, and how one person and an AI coding agent are building it.

::video dev-update-day5 | Dev update, Day 5. | portrait

It is also on YouTube, on a new channel for these updates: [Pondd Builds](https://www.youtube.com/@PonddBuilds). The channel is named after the developer and not the game, so it can outlast this one. [Watch it there](https://youtube.com/shorts/DVNdS7_Ij2o) if you would rather subscribe than check back here.

## How it was made

The same way as everything else on this site: by asking for it.

- **The script** was written from the dev log, then cut down until it fit under two minutes.
- **The voice** is the developer's own, cloned. The video says so, the YouTube description says so, and YouTube's AI label is switched on.
- **The pictures** are films and stills the project already had: the tutorial, the campaign showcase, today's five boss introductions, the portraits, a page of the fight code.
- **The edit** is a script. It lines each sentence up with the pauses in the narration, cuts a shot for it, and burns the captions in.

## What has not been checked

- The video was checked by sampling frames across it and measuring the sound level, and then on the YouTube page after it went up. Nobody sat and watched it start to finish before it was published.
- The caption timing is worked out from the pauses in the narration, so a caption may land a beat early or late.
- It says what the last post said: nobody has played today's bosses yet.
